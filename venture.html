Venture w/ Friend

The Setup

He runs a dating app. Knows paid acquisition — how to buy attention and convert it. Claims he can sell to anyone. Women convert best for him.

I build fast. AI/tech. Can ship an MVP in days.

Together: find something with proven demand, build it quick, he runs ads, we measure, we scale or kill.

Why Software

No inventory. No shipping. No suppliers. 70-90% margins. Build once, sell forever. The only cost is his ad spend and my time.

Where The Money Is

  • AI apps crossed $5B in revenue. Downloads doubled YoY.
  • Consumer subscription apps: $16B through RevenueCat alone (115K apps)
  • Health & Fitness: 2.9% download-to-paid — best conversion of any category
  • AI companion market: $48.6B, growing 31% CAGR
  • Women make 60% of social commerce purchases
  • Hard paywalls convert 5x better than freemium
  • Micro SaaS solo founders hitting $30K-$250K/mo

The Criteria

Every idea has to pass all five or we don't build it.

  • Proven demand — people already pay for this. We're not inventing a category.
  • Mechanical marketing — more spend = more revenue. Not about creative genius. Turn the crank.
  • 15 seconds — if you can't show it in a short ad, you can't sell it mechanically.
  • Recurring — subscription. LTV needs to be 3x+ acquisition cost.
  • 2-week buildable — if it takes longer, it's too complex for this model.

Noom — $600M/yr
Quiz → personalized weight plan → $59/mo. CAC $100-200. Women 25-55.

Steal: The quiz funnel. 10 questions = engagement → personalized result = trust → subscription = recurring. His ad skills map perfectly.

Co-Star — $15M/yr
Daily AI astrology content → $2.99/mo. ~5M users. Women 18-30.

Steal: Personality-driven daily content = retention crack. AI generates it free. Topic doesn't have to be astrology — anything personal + shareable.

Calm — $300M/yr
Sleep stories, meditations → $14.99/mo. Adults 25-50.

Steal: Daily habit = retention. But their content is generic. AI personalization barely used in wellness. Room to do this 10x better at 1/100th cost.

Hims/Hers — $1B+/yr
Quiz → telehealth consult → subscription products. CAC $50-80.

Steal: Medical framing = trust = willingness to pay. We'd do digital-only though — no pharmacy, no inventory.
