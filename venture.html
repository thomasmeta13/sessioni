Thesis
What are we building, for who, and why will it work

What

Software product. TBD — need to find the right thing. Criteria: proven demand, mechanical marketing, software = infinite margin.

For Who

TBD — follows from his cheapest ad audience. Candidates: women, older adults, niche professionals.

Why Us

Him: distribution, ads, funnels. Proven with dating app. Me: build speed, AI/tech, can ship MVP in days. Together: test ideas fast, kill bad ones faster.

Why Now

AI tools let 2 people build what used to take 10. Ad platforms are mature. Consumer willingness to pay for software is at all-time high ($5B+ on AI apps alone).

Rules

1. Must be software (no inventory, no shipping) 2. Must be sellable with ads mechanically (more spend = more revenue) 3. Must be explainable in 15 seconds 4. MVP in 2 weeks or less 5. $500 ad test proves/kills the idea

Open Questions
