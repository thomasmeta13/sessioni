ArthriMove 🦴

Exercise app for elderly people with arthritis — early idea stage

The Problem

  • Arthritis affects 1 in 4 adults — disproportionately elderly
  • Movement is medicine: gentle exercise reduces joint pain & stiffness
  • Existing fitness apps are built for young, able-bodied users
  • Elderly users need: large UI, voice guidance, low-impact routines, caregiver visibility

Core Features (MVP)

  • Daily guided exercise routines — chair-based, standing, range-of-motion
  • Pain check-in before/after (simple 1–5 scale)
  • Voice narration — no squinting at small text
  • Streak + gentle reminders (not aggressive)
  • Caregiver dashboard — family can see progress

Who It's For

  • Primary: elderly users 65+ with arthritis or joint pain
  • Secondary: family members / caregivers managing their routine
  • Channel: likely referral from physical therapists, senior centers

Market Angle

  • 58M arthritis sufferers in US alone
  • Most PT apps ignore the 65+ UX entirely
  • Insurance / Medicare reimbursement potential (digital therapeutics trend)
  • Subscription + B2B (senior living facilities, PT clinics)

Open Questions

  • Go direct-to-consumer or through PT / medical referral?
  • Who validates the exercise content? Partner with a PT?
  • iOS/Android or web-first?
  • AI-adaptive routines based on daily pain level?

Next Steps

  • Prototype the pain check-in flow
