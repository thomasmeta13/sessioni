SWAMP

A playable album — the game IS the music, the music IS the game

Concept

A top-down action game where you play as a frog descending through a corrupted swamp. Each zone is a track on the album. The music reacts to your combat — kill faster, the intensity rises. Slow down, it pulls back. You don't just hear the album. You play it.

"With Doom you pay $60 for an album and get a free game." — We're doing that, but with frogs.

Play the Prototype

The 4 Zones

Zone 1 — The Surface Pond

Chill, glorpy, everything's vibing. Catching bugs, learning controls. Lily pads pulse with the beat.

Sploinky / Bouncy / Wet

Zone 2 — The Bog

Frog rave cantina. DJ Sploinky on the mushroom stage. Beetle brew at the bar. The corruption is a vibe.

Rave / Glitchy / Purple

Zone 3 — The Undergrowth

Dark, dense, claustrophobic. Heavy riffs kick in. The corruption is alive and fighting back.

Heavy Riffs / Aggressive

Zone 4 — The Depths

The source. Full metal. Boss territory. This is where the album peaks.

Full Metal / Boss Fight

Combat

Tongue Whip

Main attack. Stretch, aim, snap. The core mechanic.

Swallow

Eat smaller enemies for temporary abilities or power-ups.

Belly Flop

Area damage. Jump and slam. Sends ripples that stun enemies.

Croak

Sonic attack — your voice literally layers onto the music.

Adaptive Music System

  • Each track has layers — base always playing, intensity layers kick in during combat
  • Kill fast, combo, stay aggressive = heavy layers activate
  • Boss fights have peak arrangements that only trigger when you're in the zone
  • The album release = the full peak versions (what perfect gameplay sounds like)

The player earns the full album by playing well.

Minigames

  • DOOM Mode — First-person raycaster triggered by eating a Voidfly. Psychedelic walls, tongue combat, 90 second trip.
  • Hex Puzzle — Hextris-style spore sorting. Flood-fill matching, wave patterns, chain reactions.
