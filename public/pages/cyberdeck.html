CYBERDECK

AI terminal build — content play — steampunk x green x expressive hardware

What We're Building

A custom cyberdeck — a DIY portable computer built from a Raspberry Pi, mechanical keyboard, and small display inside a rugged case. But this isn't just another Pelican case build.

This deck is alive. External LEDs and lights express what the AI is doing — thinking, listening, executing, idle. The hardware has body language. Steampunk brass + green glow aesthetic.

Why

  • Content play first — film the build, post the video
  • Cyberdecks are peaking right now (Newsweek, TikTok explosion, Gen Z obsession)
  • "AI that lives in a steampunk machine" = nobody's doing this angle yet
  • Future: sell DIY kits with OpenClaw pre-installed

Key Ideas

  • LEDs that pulse/shift color based on AI state (amber=thinking, blue=listening, green=executing)
  • Brass toggle switches, gears, leather strap handle
  • Green edge-lit acrylic panels with laser-etched circuits
  • Skeleton key as power switch
  • Analog VU meters reacting to AI activity
