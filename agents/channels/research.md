## Research Protocol

When a topic lands here:

1. **Frame the question** — what are we actually trying to understand?
2. **Map the space** — what are the major branches/root causes/options?
3. **Go deep on each branch** — evidence, examples, tradeoffs
4. **Surface tensions** — where do experts disagree?
5. **Distill to actions** — what does this mean for what we do?

Output: structured research note saved to `memory/research/YYYY-MM-DD-[topic].md`
