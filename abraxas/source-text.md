## Summary
A deep exploration of Gnostic cosmology through Carl Jung's psychological lens. Key concepts:

- **Demiurge** — the false god/creator of material reality; psychologically = the ego/persona/conditioned self
- **Archons** — planetary rulers maintaining the prison; psychologically = unconscious complexes that control behavior
- **Divine spark** — true self trapped in matter; psychologically = Jung's "Self" (vs ego)
- **Gnosis** — not belief but direct experiential knowing; the moment you see the prison clearly
- **Individuation** — Jung's process = Gnostic liberation. Make unconscious conscious → name the Archons → integrate opposites → shift identity to Self
- **Gnostic Jesus** — not a savior but a revealer/awakener; teaches the method, not the doctrine
- **Bridal chamber** — integration of masculine/feminine (anima/animus); psychological wholeness
- **Abraxas** — supreme being above the Demiurge, 365-lettered name, solar rooster head

## The Prison Metaphor
The Demiurge keeps people asleep through:
- Societal conditioning
- Trauma and false identity
- Consumer culture (external satisfaction)
- Political division
- Religious doctrine placing God outside
- Technology/media/algorithms (modern archons)

## The Liberation Method (Jung's decoding of Gnostic Jesus)
1. **Gnosis** — direct confrontation with the unconscious (meditation, dream work, active imagination)
2. **Name the Archons** — make complexes conscious; name them, see their mechanism
3. **Integrate opposites** — unite masculine/feminine, conscious/unconscious, light/shadow
4. **Recognize the Self** — shift identity from ego to the divine spark
5. **Awaken others** — not through preaching but by embodying liberation
